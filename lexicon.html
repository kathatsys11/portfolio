The Hathaway Lexicon

A register of terms

Each entry is dated, defined, and — where one exists — anchored to a permanent identifier. What is illegible to a machine does not exist to it. This is the entry, written by its author rather than inferred.

HL-001

counterfeit coherence

First published 8 July 2026

n. The feeling of pattern without the contact with reality that makes pattern true. Coherence that was never disturbed — smooth because nothing hard was ever fed into it, and therefore indistinguishable on the surface from coherence that was earned. Its danger is that it is asymptomatic: it does not present as hollow. It presents as competent.

HL-002

the Coherence Engine

First published 17 June 2026

n. A three-system framework for narrative durability. Every world that lasts runs Aliveness, Coherence, and Belonging simultaneously; when any one fails, the world dies — even where the other two are working perfectly.

→ doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21325827

HL-003

Aliveness

First published 17 June 2026

n. The condition of surplus. A world feels alive when it exceeds its own frame — when it holds histories, weather, economies, and grudges that do not exist to serve the plot but simply are. Mechanically: the world's generation of prediction error. A world that contains exactly as much as the story requires is a dead world.

HL-004

Belonging

First published 17 June 2026

n. The condition under which a world becomes a shared myth rather than a private experience. Belonging requires that a world stay general enough to be held in common. Formally, it is what regularization purchases. It is destroyed by excessive personalization — even where that personalization is technically superior.

→ doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21325827

HL-005

earned clarity

First published 3 July 2026

n. Understanding arrived at by committing to one's own read before the machine is consulted, and then checking it. Distinguished from handed clarity — a confident answer received without doing any of the interpretive work oneself. Earned clarity carries the shape of the problem it had to solve. Handed clarity carries the shape of the median.
