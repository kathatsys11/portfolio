01 / The person behind the work

A different kind of perspective.

Good technology begins with understanding the people, conditions, and constraints it must actually work for.

I spent a decade in the Army leading soldiers, commanding armored formations, teaching tactics, and working in environments where decisions have real consequences. I carried that operational perspective into large-scale logistics at Amazon, disaster operations at FEMA, and national security business operations at the Johns Hopkins Applied Physics Laboratory.

Across those roles, the common thread has been the same: make sense of complexity, build capable teams, and turn difficult problems into actionable solutions.

Today, I’m building Lead Element LLC while pursuing a joint MBA at Johns Hopkins Carey Business School and MA in Design Leadership at MICA. I bring an operator’s eye for the problem, a business perspective on execution, and a designer’s willingness to challenge assumptions.

02 / Building now

From the operational problem
to the product.

My current focus is practical protection and survivability technology for people and critical systems operating in contested environments.

Founder & CEO · Maryland-based defense technology

Lead Element

Lead Element develops protection and survivability solutions informed by operational experience and built around real-world constraints: mobility, usability, adaptability, and the ability to perform under pressure.

Its first product in development, Sponson, is a rapid-deployment multispectral hide-site concept for small teams. The design prioritizes quick setup and stowage, low-profile signature management, and operator utility.
