The through-line

Before any decision gets made, there’s a moment when the picture is still forming. That moment is where I work.

It started in cavalry formations — going where things were unclear, piecing together what was actually happening, and getting it back to where it mattered in time. The terrain has changed since: FEMA disaster operations, Amazon logistics at scale, business intelligence for a national security mission, and now building with AI. The instinct hasn’t. Take something tangled and moving, give it structure, and turn it into a choice someone can make.

Published analysis · white paper

Accuracy Before Tempo

A white paper on the defense industrial base: why wartime production can’t be ordered into existence. It names the failure mode — the Output Control Fallacy — and shows that speed is a lagging indicator of upstream engineering discipline, not a lever you can push. Written months before the June 2026 GAO assessment confirmed the mechanism (100+ major programs, 12+ years to a first capability). A call made early, on the record — and it came true.
